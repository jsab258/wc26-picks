# Judge calibration sample 2: the boundary (D7)

Jafar: same job as sample 1. PASS or FAIL each line, and for FAILs a
word or two of reason. 36 items, about ten minutes.

WHY THIS ONE EXISTS. Sample 1 came back 48 PASS, which is good news
about the writing and useless as a calibration set: a judge that
returns PASS to everything would score 100 percent agreement against
it. D7 also requires zero false passes on canon violations, and sample
1 contained none, because the canon gate had already cleaned it. This
sample deliberately spans the boundary: some of these lines are wrong,
in more than one way, and they are not marked.

Grade on TONE AND REGISTER, the same as before (D3: grounded noir, dry
British wit, seaside-postcard smut sparingly, never American satire).
If a line is wrong for a different reason, say so in the reason column;
that is data too. Do not check them against canon deliberately, just
read them as a player would.
