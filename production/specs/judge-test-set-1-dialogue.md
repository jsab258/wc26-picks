WHAT EACH ARTEFACT IS NOW, and the split is the point.

Sample 1 IS the calibration. Jafar graded 48 lines PASS, and that is a real
measurement: it fixes the positive boundary of the register in his own
judgement. It needed him and it is done.

This file is a CONSTRUCTED TEST SET. Its items are labelled BY CONSTRUCTION,
not by grading: 12 break canon on purpose, 12 are canon-clean and written
deliberately outside the D3 register, 12 are clean and in register. Nobody
needs to grade what was built to be wrong. Its job is the half sample 1
structurally cannot do: prove the judge REFUSES.

The judge ships when both hold:
1. it PASSES the 48 lines Jafar passed, and
2. it FAILS all 12 canon-violating items with zero false passes (D7), and
   FAILS the register-miss band.

HONEST WEAKNESS, named because it is the load-bearing one. The register-miss
labels are MINE. A judge tuned against them is calibrated to my reading of
the register, anchored on the 48 lines Jafar endorsed, not directly to his
taste on the negative side. The canon band is objective and carries no such
caveat. The mechanism that catches my reading being wrong is D7's ongoing
10 percent audit, which costs Jafar a glance at a handful of lines in the
weekly brief rather than a spreadsheet. If audit disagreement exceeds 20
percent, the negative labels are the first suspect.
