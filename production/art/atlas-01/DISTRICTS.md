# Seven district proposals

Every sheet contains an establishing image, a player-height image, materials and characteristic objects. These are AI concept proposals, not reference photographs or rendered geometry. The companion map sheets share the atlas coordinates. Numerical dimensions and placement remain in the drawn/data sources; incidental AI detail is not an asset specification.

Continuation: [evidence-driven detail studies](previews/evidence-revisions.png), [work/home routes](previews/town-work-and-home.png) and [evidence ledger](references/CONTINUATION.md) qualify these retained images. No replacement concept image or new render was generated this pass. The table below makes their social and physical scope explicit; it does not turn them into canon.

| District | 1990 fictional work/home pattern and transition | What the image does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Packing, repair, shop work and households above shops; maintained metal refits among brick. Quay Street leads to market; gates serve receiving and returns | A whole port cannot be all damp heritage fronts. New dry-day grocery study adds everyday trade, R05/R08 |
| Copper Row | Independent food/hardware shops, stalls, barber and bookmaker; bus connects shoppers and shop workers to outside station/supermarket | Market activity is a proposal, not a verified timetable; individual interiors remain to author, R19 |
| Exchange | Lawyers, harbour-related offices and public service counters; post-war replacements among older offices; clerks reach cinema via Civic Rise | Formal spaces do not prove wealth of every resident or office occupant; furnishings and records handling need sources |
| Parade | Cinema, drink trade, taxi calls, occasional contemporary cafe/wine-bar refits; daytime cleaning/deliveries precede night queues | Contemporary refits are possible (R12), not a licence for generic modern hospitality or invented branded neon |
| Fairview | Upper-hill houses/terraces plus lower Foundry Court flats, washing court, school and bus; retained 45m crest | Existing concept represents upper hill. R06/R07 and Plymouth R11 justify a wider household/terrain study, not the fictional tenure or dates |
| Ironside | Active repair sheds and receiving beside obsolete plant; public route stays outside work gates; freight goes west, families walk uphill | Retort is not uniformly abandoned; disused diagonal track does not imply all freight stopped, R08/R10 |
| Gullwing | Boarding-house owners, seasonal/service workers, arcade visitors; laundry yard and sea-wall route have different access | Faded resort is still inhabited and trading. Exact seasonal schedules, domestic rooms and modernisation need dated resort evidence |

## The Hook

Low working quays and deep shop plots. Mickey's is the first east-side bay on Quay Street; the fish shop follows north. The basin is south, market uphill north. Delivery and evening regulars make repeated contact plausible.

## Copper Row

The market hall sits beside Weighhouse Lane, between the Hook and Fairview. Folded stalls preserve access after trading; barbers and betting offices supply different reasons to linger. Conversations here should mix people from other districts.

## The Exchange

Records Court cafe faces formal offices on the northeast inland shelf. Police and legal offices are separate named destinations. Glazing and venetian blinds make observation asymmetric; a queue at the counter can reveal a visit without revealing its purpose.

## The Parade

The Tivoli marks a nightlife route between the coast and the civic quarter. Tile, small neon and poster cases distinguish the evening street from the Hook. Queues and taxi telephone notices make slow, visible transitions between venues.

![The Parade AI concept](concepts/parade.png)

## Fairview

Contour-following residential terraces rise inland to the north and west. The school chapel and steps are the named landmark. Garden gates, retaining walls and upper windows divide access from observation; school meetings are design intentions.

![Fairview AI concept](concepts/fairview.png)

## Ironside

Workshops and loading yards occupy the western industrial edge. The establishing view looks east, with hills left/north and docks right/south. The Retort works stack and Clocking-in club tie shift routines to Tannery Row. Gates have work destinations.

## Gullwing

A low sea-wall shelf on the eastern waterfront carries boarding houses, Winter Rooms arcade and a short pier. Rising inland roofs continue toward Fairview. Faded teal and salt render belong to leisure businesses still operating, not a universal ruin set.
