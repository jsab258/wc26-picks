# Mickey's: a small pub with a long memory

Proposal for the owner's visual approval. Tom Novak inherits Mickey's from his uncle Mickey; that canon remains the reason this is the player's place. The following building history is new art, not an asserted canon addition.

An approximately 1880 chandler's shop became a beer house around 1925. The separate household entrance survived the conversion, explaining the two adjacent source doors. A cheap brick WC was built in the yard around 1958. A 1970s counter and low snug screen sit awkwardly within the older room rather than making the shell conveniently wider. Repairs are economical: a patched lino strip where staff stand, older carpet under the snug, a polished counter edge and less cared-for skirting behind the beer rack. The pub is maintained by people with little spare cash.

The place occupies east_parade_bay0 on the existing Hook street, source x=3..9m, z=5.125..13.125m. The 42m street, 6m carriageway, two 2m footways, east and west block positions and west x=21..24m service gap are retained. The word east_parade in the piece IDs is not a move into the Parade district. The fish shop is the next bay north. Facing the pub from the west, north is left: glazing, public entrance, then the narrower private entrance on the right.

The public door leads into the front room, then past the counter to the rear door. The bar runs along the north side; its 1.15m staff aisle leads independently to a service door. A small ground-level beer store avoids inventing an unprovided cellar. Deliveries are hand-trolley loads from the basin approach, through the south terrace-end passage, along the rear lane and through the public rear door to the store before opening. Delivery is a chosen daily meeting opportunity, not a current schedule. A van should not enter the 2.4m rear lane.

The snug bench faces the bar behind a 1.45m screen. From the pavement, a hand at the front table could be seen through the glazing while a hand behind the screen is occluded. A standing head may still be seen. The marked seat is a plausible place to overhear bar talk, subject to real acoustics, speaker timing, attention and recognition. Curtains and reflections can frustrate the view; an architectural sightline grants no witness fact. A regular might remember a coat or delivery time without identifying the act. Staff, delivery workers and evening regulars have reasons to meet again here, allowing memory to become conversation if integration supports it.

The escape intention runs through the public rear door, round the WC, through the 1.2m yard gate, south along the rear lane and west down the terrace-end passage. It does not cross the fish shop. The 1.8m yard wall is overlooked by upper windows. This route changes the encounter; it is not an invisibility corridor. Customer, staff, trolley and escape lines have been checked in the drawing against a conservative 0.30m-radius footprint. Door movement and actual navigation remain untested.

Upstairs is a completely authored household: front bedroom, living room, rear book room, kitchen and bath. The private stair has 19 rises to +3.4m, 18 goings of .24m and an explicit floor opening. The compact stair begins .30m inside the threshold. The private door is proposed to open outward so its leaf does not pass through the stair; that puts a .838m sweep on the 2m footway. This is an explicit design tradeoff for visual and navigation review, not a building-code approval. The recipe includes named proxy furnishings and all five preview cameras. It is a spatial blockout with opaque proxy glazing, not finished game art. Exact coordinates live in data/mickeys.json; pictures must not override them.
