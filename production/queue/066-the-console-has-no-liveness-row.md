## Why it exists, in one incident

On the morning of 3 September a run was announced as dispatched and had never
started. The silence that followed looked exactly like work in progress, and
an hour went into explaining the slowness of a run that did not exist. Under
the Producer register that hour is correctly SILENT, because none of it was
worth Jafar's attention. Silence being an acceptable exit is right, and it
means the console has to carry the liveness that the channel no longer does.

## The distinction that makes it useful

WHEN THE STUDIO LAST DID SOMETHING REAL, not when the page was rebuilt. A
regenerator running every fifteen minutes over a dead studio produces a page
that looks freshly alive, which is the fault the dashboard already carries a
checkout-age Reading to avoid. Landed commits, landed runs and ruled cards are
things; a rebuild is not.

## Not in scope

No push. This row is pulled, never sent: a liveness signal that pages Jafar is
a heartbeat, and heartbeats are banned from the register by his own ruling.
