## Why this is the most important item on the board

The whole project rests on one claim: that people who genuinely perceive,
remember and gossip make a town feel alive in a way scripted responses do not.
EVERY OTHER NUMBER IN THIS REPOSITORY ASSUMES IT. Nobody has tested it.

Jafar's own framing: "If they cannot perceive a difference, that is the most
important finding this project can produce."

That sentence is the acceptance criterion. THIS ITEM IS NOT ALLOWED TO COME OUT
WELL BY CONSTRUCTION, which makes it the opposite of the group in queue 117.

## The design, and the parts that matter

- ONE crime. Not a tour, not a sandbox. The smallest thing that can propagate.
- THE EXISTING BUILD. No new content, no new systems. If it needs building, it
  is the wrong test.
- THREE UNBRIEFED PEOPLE. Unbriefed is load bearing: someone told what to look
  for will find it. They are not told the two runs differ, or how.
- BOTH ARMS. Real propagation, and fixed canned responses that are plausible
  and not deliberately bad. A straw arm proves nothing.
- ORDER VARIES between participants, because whichever comes second benefits
  from familiarity with the controls.

## What it must not become

Not a demo. Not a feel check by Jafar, who cannot be unbriefed about his own
game. Not a metric collected by the sim about itself: the reading is what a
person says, unprompted, about which felt more alive.

## The honest outcomes, all three named in advance

1. They can tell, and say why in terms that match the mechanism. The moat is
   real and the project's premise survives its first contact with a stranger.
2. They cannot tell. THE MOST IMPORTANT FINDING THIS PROJECT CAN PRODUCE, and
   it is reported plainly and immediately, not softened and not re-run until it
   comes out differently.
3. They can tell but prefer the canned arm. That is a third answer nobody has
   considered and it would be worth more than the first.

## The blocker to name honestly

Three unbriefed people is a Jafar problem, not a studio one: the studio cannot
recruit anyone. What the studio can do is make the two arms, the scenario and
the asking script so that his part is an evening, not a project. Say plainly in
the report what he has to do and how long it takes.
