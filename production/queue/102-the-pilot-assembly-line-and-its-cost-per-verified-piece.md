## The number this whole plan rests on, and nobody has measured it

Phase 0's exit gate says the pilot line yields a verified piece. Every estimate
about how long the content of this game takes is downstream of the cost of one
verified piece, and that cost has never been measured once.

## Step one is a CHOICE and it is not a builder's

The studio chooses the content type and says why. The criteria, both binding:

1. THE PILOT CAN RUN IT END TO END THIS WEEK. Not most of it, not with the
   verify step deferred. A type whose verifier does not exist is a type that
   will produce an authoring cost and no verified piece.
2. THE COST PER VERIFIED PIECE MUST GENERALISE. A type whose cost is dominated
   by something unique to it teaches nothing about the next type.

Candidates that exist in this tree today, listed so the ruling has something to
compare and NOT as a recommendation: dialogue lines, which have a spec
(`production/specs/dialogue-pub-regular-v1.md`), a judge calibration and a
graded sample; props through the mesh pipeline; decals and images through
imagegen; street kit pieces; NPC schedules. Do not touch
`content/dialogue/pub-regular-v1.json`: those 48 lines are the graded judge
calibration sample and changing one invalidates it silently.

The ruling is written before authoring starts, names the criteria it applied,
and is stamped as a ruling.

## The trap that would make the number a lie

The free lane runs on Jafar's PC and costs ZERO points. If the chosen type's
authoring step is free-lane, the cost per verified piece is dominated by the
spec, verify and integrate steps, and quoting one total would generalise
straight into a wrong plan for a type with no free lane. So the cost is
reported SPLIT BY STEP with the free-lane steps named as free.

## The calibration it rests on, and its three named weaknesses

`production/budget.md` carries the live calibration: a spawn costs roughly 1.5
to 2 points, derived 2026-09-02, and it is weak three ways: it averages two
populations whose turn counts differ by 3.75x, its denominator counted spawns
that produced no turn at all (148 of 170 limit-noticed transcripts produced
none), and it counted 240 logged rows against 454 transcripts. Turns are not
points and the conversion is UNMEASURED.

So the cost here comes from METER READINGS either side of the run, both meters,
with the window stated. Budget.md's own rule is that the only clean rate this
project has ever had was an overnight window in which Jafar was asleep. A
window he worked in is contaminated and its delta is two quantities added
together.

## Both halves, accepting first

Accepting: the ruling, then the run, then the number with its split, its two
readings and its window.

Rejecting: a run whose verify step passed nothing prints `verified=0/M` and
"nothing measured" for the cost. A pipeline that authored ten pieces and
verified none has measured the cost of authoring, which is a different quantity
with a similar name.
