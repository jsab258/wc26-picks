## The shape, which is the point of grouping these

EVIDENCE THAT CANNOT DISAGREE WITH US. Four instances, and they are not four
separate mistakes:

1. `agency-model.md` scores this project 93 against competitors at 60, and that
   SELF-RATING IS CITED IN D12 AS A PREMISE. A score we gave ourselves became
   an input to a decision.
2. THE JUDGE WAS CALIBRATED ON 48 PASSES AND ZERO FAILS. A judge that has never
   seen a fail has not been shown to distinguish anything. Its agreement number
   is a measure of nothing until it can disagree.
3. QUEUE 111: the studio-versus-game split counted AGENT TYPES rather than what
   was built, and reported twelve game sessions when one was, on the most
   studio-heavy day this project has had. The metric built to catch the studio
   maintaining itself instead of improving the project could not fire.
4. The soak citation, queue 116, is the same family: an instrument that said
   NOT GATED being read as support.

Every one of these produced a number that FLATTERED and that no available
evidence could contradict.

## The three rules Jafar ruled, and they are the deliverable

- MARK EVERY SELF-RATED SCORE AS JUDGEMENT wherever it is cited. Not once at
  its source: at every citation, because the citation is where it does harm.
- NEVER LET ONE JUSTIFY REDUCED INVESTMENT. A self-rating saying we are already
  ahead is the most expensive kind of wrong.
- ADD NEGATIVE CASES TO JUDGE CALIBRATION BEFORE ANY FURTHER DEPLOYMENT. Both
  counts printed together, always; a pass count alone is what got us here.

## The trap in fixing it

Do not relabel and stop. A score marked JUDGEMENT that still sits in a
decision's premise line is still deciding things. Where a self-rating is load
bearing, either replace it with something that could have come out badly, or
record that the decision rests on judgement and say whose.

## Both halves

Accepting: the live tree, swept, with every citation carrying its label and the
count printed.
Rejecting: a planted self-rated score cited bare is caught. And for the judge:
a calibration set with zero fails is REFUSED by whatever check this lands,
because that is the exact state being repaired.
