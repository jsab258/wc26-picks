## What the audit said, and it is a claim about us

Its affordable target: ONE BLOCK, A PUB, TWO INTERIORS, TWELVE RESIDENTS, and a
60 to 90 minute crime-and-gossip scenario. Its cost model puts the stated 300
to 500 resident town at 14 TO 34 WEEKS OF FULL BUDGET ALLOCATION FOR CONTENT
ALONE.

That is an outside estimate of our throughput. It may be wrong. THE ONLY WAY TO
FIND OUT IS TO PRODUCE OURS, from data, and set the two side by side.

## Why this cannot be answered today, and that is the finding

`production/budget.md` says the turns-to-points conversion is UNMEASURED and
the per-spawn figure is a flat average over populations 3.75x apart. Queue 076
prices a token; queue 092 prices a wake; neither prices a PIECE. So the number

## The rules this number must obey, because it will be quoted

- Name the calibration and its date, per the standing rule that no estimate is
  printed bare.
- Say which statistic it is: median, mean, at-worst. A cost per piece that is a
  mean over two populations is the fault this project has already recorded.
- Ship the denominator: how many verified pieces the figure rests on. If it is
  one, say one. Note queue 118: one of the pieces currently counted has a
  pending gate, so the denominator may be smaller than the count suggests.
- DO NOT AVERAGE OURS WITH THE AUDIT'S. If they differ, the difference is the
  interesting thing and it gets explained.
