## The trap in the obvious fix, and why this task is an inventory first

DO NOT simply copy one list into the other. THEY ANSWER DIFFERENT QUESTIONS
and a blanket sync would be wrong: a rendered verdict file can be evidence for
review purposes while its staleness genuinely does invalidate a footer that
was computed before it changed. The comment now in `verify-gate.sh` saying the
two lists "have to be kept in step" overstates it, and that sentence is itself
a claim to check rather than to obey.

What is actually needed is the inventory: every path, every guard, the answer
each guard gives, and a NAMED REASON wherever two guards differ. A difference
with a reason is a design. A difference with no reason is the bug this task
exists for.
