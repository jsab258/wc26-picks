## The finding

E10 is MANDATORY in the bill of materials, has no prompt in the generator,
and canon has minted three street names since 2 September: Quay Street,
Weighhouse Lane and Tannery Row. So the one object in the street that would
tell a player where they are does not exist, and the plate renders blank.

A blank plate reads as a bug rather than as an absence, which is why this is
content work rather than a note. Quay Street is in the Hook, which is the
district the vignette sits in, so that is the name unless the director says
otherwise.
