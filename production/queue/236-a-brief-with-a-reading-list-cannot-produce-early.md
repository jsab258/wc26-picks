  THE RULE THAT EXISTS. Jafar, 2026-09-10: every builder brief opens with
  "produce at a clean boundary first, refine second". It is on every brief since
  and the fault kept happening: 53 calls and no diff, 54 and no diff, 56 and no
  ruling, 45 and a half-written test block, all on 10 September.

  WHY IT IS NOT ENOUGH. The same briefs name three to five documents to read IN
  FULL before starting. A reasonable agent obeys the reading list first, because
  it was told to, and the budget is gone before the first edit. The two
  instructions are in direct conflict and the reading list wins because it is
  concrete.

  THE SECOND HALF, WHICH IS ABOUT CONTENT AND NOT LENGTH. A brief asked for a
  pinned exposure value to be derived from a printed series. The series is output
  luma from a tonemapped frame; the setting is a scene-luminance input; there is
  no arithmetic between them, and this project had already written down that the
  adapted exposure is never read by this process. THE BRIEF ASKED FOR SOMETHING
  UNREACHABLE and the agent that produced nothing was behaving correctly.

  THE PROPOSED RULE, in two parts. FACTS INLINE, at most one required document,
  everything else offered rather than required. And BEFORE ASKING FOR A NUMBER,
  check it can be reached from what the agent will have; if it cannot, ask for
  the measurement that would produce it instead.

  IT NEEDS A RULING because it changes how every spawn is written, which is
  process and not code, and the resident should not rule on the quality of its
  own briefs.
