## The finding

`map.html` renders the area "the people and their memory" as HARNESS ONLY with
12 of 12 gates ok and `gossipHeat=0.90 knownLeads=16 witnesses=9`. Every one of
those numbers is true and the area reads as healthy.

The sweep landed the same day and measured, over 1,296 sessions and the whole
108-path space, `lieHeard=0/90` and `liveSilent=1944/1944`: the mechanism fires
and nothing the player can hear ever changes, and in the shipped build the
recognition beats are silent in 648 of 648 sessions.

SO THE MAP TELLS HIM THE MOAT IS FINE ON THE DAY THE STUDIO PROVED IT IS
INAUDIBLE. That is the comforting bar the map exists to refuse, and it is
refused correctly for the street and not for memory, because the street's
required keys are absent while memory's twelve gates all answer ok.

## Why the gates cannot see it

Those twelve gates measure whether the simulation RAN: heat moved, leads
exist, witnesses filed. Not one of them asks whether a player could HEAR the
result, because until today nothing in this repository measured that. The
gates are not wrong. They answer a different question from the one the area's
one-word state implies.

## What the fix is

The sweep now writes committed numbers under `production/stranger-test/`. The
memory area should require `lieHeard` and `liveSilent` the way the street area
requires the compile keys, and read them from there. Until it does, the area
is answering "did the machinery turn" while the page's heading promises "what
state is this part of the game in".

## The rule this is an instance of

A number that answers a different question from the one it is printed under is
the same fault as a missing denominator. Both let a reader draw a conclusion
the measurement does not carry. The street area got this right on the first
try today; memory did not, and nobody noticed until a message had to cite it.
