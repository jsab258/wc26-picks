# Scope v2 (2026-08-31)

## Phase A: the town (the game until further notice)
- Playable core: about 1 km2 of dense town: harbour front, high street, terraces, industrial edge.
- People: 300 to 500 residents, all promotable to full simulation by attention, all named, scheduled, and remembered by the mill. A smaller named cast carries the narrative.
- Interiors: Tier 1, 10 to 20 authored hero interiors with full persistent state (pub, police station, betting shop, key homes and shops). Tier 2, 50 to 150 procedural interiors from a modular room grammar, persisted as seed plus delta. Tier 3, every remaining window gets a fake-interior shader. Experiment behind a perf gate: promotion by attention (any door forceable, Tier 2 interior generated on first entry, delta kept forever).
- Venues (each must be a place where information moves): pub, betting shop, dog track, barbershop, VHS rental, arcade, chippy, working men's club, church, the football ground on match days.
- Play length target: 20 to 40 hours from systems plus story.
- One systemic work vehicle (a van) as an object for jobs, not a driving experience.

## Phase B: the region (gated on Phase 3 and 4 exits)
- 10 to 15 km2: coast road, docks hinterland, a second settlement, countryside between. For reference, KCD2's whole acclaimed world is about 16 km2.
- Driving and traffic enter here, and only here, because driving is only meaningful at region scale. Vehicle physics bought or reused, never built.

## Cut list (reopening any item requires a decision record)
Multiplayer and netcode. GTA-scale landmass. Environmental destructibility. Euphoria-class physics and animation parity. Modern-day tech (mobiles, internet, social media). Real brands, likenesses, voices, licensed music. Character customization beyond outfits and hair. Forced save friction as default (optional hardcore mode only). Monetization, platforms, localization, moderation until ship-prep.
