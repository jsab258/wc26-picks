# Reference extraction: what LEDGER adopts (one-page rollup)

Cheap per-session read. Detail and rationale live in research/gta6-triage.md (steal, adapt, skip, plus era corrections) and research/kcd2-rdr2-summary.md (adopt, reject, failure modes). Full reports in research/full/.

ADOPTED SURFACES AND VERBS (mostly from GTA 6)
1. The what-they-know HUD over the seven slots and the identification ladder.
2. Pre-crime prep verbs: disguise slots, weapon concealment, clean getaway vehicle, route choice.
3. Petty crime verb set (small robberies as repeatable systemic crimes); witnesses with a courage value who may intervene.
4. Eavesdropping is itself perceived; contextual verbs on anyone (greet, intimidate, rob, mock) as the button layer under typed and spoken input.
5. Cash on person vs bank, physically distinct; era car theft (coat hanger, hotwire QTE, smash, method sets the noise event).
6. Barber as gossip hub; dogs as sensors; the town reacts to itself (breakdowns, deliveries, bin day, funerals); out-of-area vehicles implying a wider world; pet the dog.

ADOPTED SYSTEMS (mostly from KCD2 and RDR2)
1. NPC AI LOD tiers (full sim near, coarse schedule ticks far; limits set early).
2. GOAP plus behavior trees where fitting; per-NPC daycycle schedules authored as data.
3. Witness runs to the phone box or station; intercept, bribe, threaten or kill; chains when witnesses are silenced.
4. Punishment escalation ending in a visible social mark; never a wipe; remediation is behavioral.
5. Reputation derived from remembered events, per person and per faction; surfaced, never stored as a global number.
6. Needs and friction loops; time-sensitive and self-resolving quests (the world proceeds without you).
7. Household and pub morale plus contribution counters, with memory-conditioned chatter.
8. Encounter skeletons with trigger conditions; generation fills variation to fight repetition.
9. Moving world furniture on schedules (buses, milk floats, brewery drays); the local paper reports deeds.

ADOPTED CONTENT PROGRAM
1. Brand and media bible: fictional bands, adverts, TV, pirate radio, the football club, pun shopfronts, all in the D3 register.
2. Era-styled original soundtrack via allowlisted generation; DJ patter and adverts written and voiced at build time.

REJECTED (reopening any needs a decision record)
Global morality scalar. Felony wipes and expiring wanted states. Animation and physics parity with mocap studios. Destructibility. Procedural filler people. Modern-day tech. Licensed music, real brands, likenesses. Forced save friction as default. Map-scale chasing.
