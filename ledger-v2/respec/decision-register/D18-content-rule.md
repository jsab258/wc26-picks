# D18: the content rule, permanent

STATUS: DECIDED 2026-09-10 by Jafar, dictated and recorded by the resident.
Premise level and PERMANENT. It governs what may exist in this work at all,
in image and in speech, and it extends D17 rather than replacing it.

## The rule, as given, in his order

- **Alcohol and gambling out entirely**, per D17.
- **Tobacco stays.**
- **Violence stays**, including blood and light gore. NO TORTURE OR CRUELTY
  AS SPECTACLE.
- **Killing is possible, rare, permanent, AND THE TOWN REMEMBERS IT FOREVER.**
- **Full period swearing allowed. NO SLURS OF ANY KIND.**
- **Drugs exist as an off-screen economy others run**, never shown, never
  used, never a player verb.
- **No prostitution, no sexual content.**
- **NO CHILDREN ANYWHERE**: none rendered, none in the crowd, none in
  dialogue or image prompts. THE SCHOOL STANDS CLOSED for the game's window.
- **Racism and sectarianism may exist as facts about characters**, never
  voiced as slurs, never rewarded.
- **Religion present as part of life**, never mocked, never a mechanic.
- **Police corruptible as individuals, never as a thesis.**

## Where it is enforced, all five

1. canon.md, as a rule.
2. Every image spec's content clause.
3. The word-list gate over dialogue and spoken lines.
4. **Crowd generation**, which is new with D18 and is the one enforcement
   site that is code rather than text: a crowd that samples ages cannot be
   allowed to sample a child.
5. The brand bible.

And: run the gate over everything existing and report what it catches.

## What it costs immediately, named rather than discovered later

**THE LANDED ART CONTAINS CHILDREN AND A SCHOOL.** The outside Fairview sheet
has two children on the steps and a FAIRVIEW SCHOOL sign. The atlas carries
F1 Chapel and school gate as a Fairview landmark. The Copper Row spec names a
school satchel among its objects. None of that survives D18 unchanged, and
the school being CLOSED is the form the rule gives: the building stands, the
children do not.

That is a rule about the WORLD and not only about the prompt, so it reaches
the atlas and the district sheets, not just the word list.

## The line this rule draws, which is the hard part

Several of these permit the FACT and forbid the DEPICTION or the REWARD:
racism as a character fact but never as a voiced slur; drugs as an economy
but never a verb; police corruptible as individuals but never as a thesis.
A gate that cannot tell a fact about a character from a slur in their mouth
will either mangle ordinary writing or pass the thing it exists to stop, and
getting that boundary right is the work rather than the word list.
