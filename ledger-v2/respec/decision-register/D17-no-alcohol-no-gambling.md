# D17: no alcohol and no gambling, anywhere

STATUS: DECIDED 2026-09-10 by Jafar, dictated and recorded by the resident.
Premise level: it is a content rule over the whole work, in image and in
speech, and it voids decisions already taken.

## The rule, as given

**Alcohol is never shown, served, drunk or spoken of, and gambling likewise,
anywhere, in image or speech. PUBS MAY EXIST AS PLACES.**

## Where it is enforced, all four

1. **canon.md**, as a rule and not a note.
2. **Every image spec's content clause**, beside the existing bar on real
   trade marks and identifiable people.
3. **A word-list gate over dialogue banks and spoken lines.**
4. **The brand bible.**

## What it voids, named so nobody rebuilds them

The pub-specific decisions that depend on drink are VOID: the beer store,
the pump clips, the cask line, and the free-house question.

**The siting, the two bays and the fascia STAND.** They are architecture and
they do not depend on what is served.

NOTHING PUB-SPECIFIC IS BUILT until ruling 6 resolves, because ruling 6 may
replace the pub with a minicab office and building a pub interior first would
be work done twice.

## The design problem it creates, which is real and is not a formality

A pub the game never shows drinking in needs a written case for what people
are doing inside it, and the Parade's nightlife identity has to be redesigned
without drink AS A DESIGN TASK WITH PICTURES rather than as a subtraction.
Both are named in ruling 6's commission.

## What is not added to the inventory because of this

Drunkenness, pub games and gambling are not added as systems, per the same
message's inventory instruction.
