# D5: Interiors, three tiers plus promotion by attention
Date: 2026-08-31. Status: APPROVED, experiment gated.
Choice: Tier 1, 10 to 20 authored hero interiors, full persistent state. Tier 2, 50 to 150 from a modular room grammar, persisted as seed plus delta (store the generation seed and only the player-made differences). Tier 3, fake-interior shaders on every remaining window. Experiment: promotion by attention, any door forceable, Tier 2 interior generated on first entry, delta kept forever; ships only if it holds the perf gate set at its milestone kickoff.
Rationale: matches the existing philosophy (the town persists as a seed plus its exceptions) applied to space; gives every-building-enterable, which GTA 6 does not claim, as lazy generation rather than content cost.
