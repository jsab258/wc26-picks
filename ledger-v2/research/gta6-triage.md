# GTA 6 triage (Extended Look 2026-08-27 plus IGN 99-details transcript), consolidated

Pattern found: GTA 6's most-praised systems are shallow global versions of the perception engine, with excellent player-facing surfaces. The steals are mostly surfaces and verbs on top of systems LEDGER already runs deeper.

STEAL (adopted)
1. The what-they-know HUD: icons for CCTV, clothes known, face seen, vehicle known, seen-as-couple, weapon; white-to-red stars for pursued vs wanted; hollow stars for responding-without-description. Becomes the player-facing surface of the seven slots and the ladder.
2. Pre-crime prep verbs: masks and disguise slots, concealment before entering, clean getaway vehicle, route choice. Player controls over the perceivable slots.
3. Petty crime verb set: small shop robberies as repeatable systemic crimes.
4. Civilians who act: a witness grabbed the robber's gun. Courage value on witnesses; have-a-go heroes.
5. Eavesdropping noticed by NPCs (perception pointed at the player).
6. Contextual verbs on anyone (greet, intimidate, rob, mock) as the button layer under typed/spoken input.
7. Cash on person vs bank, physically distinct; bankers' hours; the pub till launders.
8. Brand and media bible: recurring fictional brands, bands, adverts, TV, teams (see D3 register). Includes the football club, pirate radio, regional TV, pun shopfronts.
9. Barber as gossip hub; hair and stubble grow.
10. Non-lethal surfaced in combat UI (disarm highlights).
11. Car theft simplified to era: coat hanger, hotwire QTE, or smash (loud). Method sets the noise event.
12. Out-of-area vehicles implying a wider world.
13. Pet the dog; dogs bark at strangers and sneaks (sensors).
14. The town reacts to itself: breakdowns, deliveries, bin day, a funeral, with responder NPCs.

ADAPT
1. Criminal profile: never a stored global meter; surfaced reputation derived from the belief network.
2. NPCs filming fights: era version is gawkers-as-witnesses plus the local paper photographer; a photo in Tuesday's paper is a fact in the mill. One camcorder in town as a rare hard-evidence witness.
3. Metabolism: appearance as information; NPCs notice you look rough and tell each other.
4. Public affection feeds the seen-as-couple slot.
5. Activities small and period (darts, pool, pier fishing, dog track, bare-knuckle at the docks); each must be an information venue.
6. Escalation tiers legible (six-star lesson) on the existing law chain.
7. One postcard view per district, inside frame budget.

SKIP
Modern social media and influencers (paper, radio, payphones are better for the design). Map scale and 80-hour breadth. Destructibility. Slow-mo focus meter (defer to combat spec). Character switching. Licensed soundtrack (fictional bands instead).

ERA CORRECTIONS (canonical, late 80s to early 90s)
CCTV exists and is rare (bank, off-licence, weekly tape recycling; tapes are stealable objects). Pagers for dealers and fixers, callback from phone boxes. Ram-raiding and TWOC/joyriding are period crime verbs. Pirate radio; Madchester, synthpop, fading punk, early rave for the fictional bands. VHS rental and arcade as venues; teletext on the pub telly; satellite dish on the pub. Match-day culture, casuals included.

NOTE: Rockstar states no generative AI was used in GTA 6 and ships no runtime AI characters. The moat is uncontested.
