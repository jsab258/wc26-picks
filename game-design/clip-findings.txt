I THEN TRIED TO CORROBORATE IT FROM THE FBX AND THE CORROBORATION DOES NOT
HOLD, which is the part worth writing down. `lie_still` measures 2.18m of
travel in 1.67s and I published that as "a walking speed, so it is not a
lying-down animation". Reading the whole column kills it:

  Walking                    travel 0.00      Female Walk   travel 1.22
  Standing React Large Gut   travel 2.29      Lying Down    travel 2.18
  Jog Forward                travel 0.00      Running       travel 1.67

A standing reaction does not travel 2.29m in 0.73s (3.1 m/s), and one walk
reading 0.00 beside another reading 1.22 is not a property of walks. So the
column does not track what the animation IS, and cannot be used to argue about
what a clip contains. `measure()` takes the HIPS' local translation curves,
which is right only while the hips are unparented and carry the world motion;
that assumption is the thing to check.

  OPEN: clip-motion's travel/path columns are not trustworthy for this
  question. Nothing else in the project reads them, and the duplicate and
  frozen-root findings — which are what `clipFindings` counts — do not depend
  on them.
