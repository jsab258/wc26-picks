# Act II — The Squeeze (APPROVED 2026-07-26: 1a Facts+one mechanical effect, 2a highest-loyalty evening, 3a flat $600 truce, 4a Table resolves the act)

Drafted 2026-07-26. Per doc §8: growth attracts the three rivals and one
investigator, and the two lives begin colliding through the gossip system.
Act II's set pieces are systemic collisions the spine guarantees. Same
design bet as Act I: the empire machinery we built IS the act's skeleton;
authored meaning lays over it. All pressure points fire on CONDITIONS —
no dates, no timers (§4).

**Act II opens** when the city is open AND the empire is real: two of
(a business owned, a racket established, crew of 2+). The question Act I
asked was *what did you inherit*; Act II's question is *how big can this
get before the lives touch* — and the act guarantees they touch.

## Pressure points

**PP1 — Three Slow Beers (fires: any second arm reaches attention 0.25).**
Act II's opening image: it isn't one rival anymore. Authored: one line per
arm's first notice, in its doctrine's voice (Sera's man drinks; Aldous's
clerk photographs the deed; Danny's kid tags the wall).

**PP2 — A Lawyer's Letter (fires: machine attention ≥ 0.5).**
The machine's version of the street rent: an injunction freezes the BAR's
takings (not the fronts') for two days unless answered — pay the fee,
or ask the Fixer to make it disappear, or eat the loss. Introduces Aldous
by proxy: you never see him; you see paper with his firm's letterhead.

**PP3 — The Kid (fires: New crew attention ≥ 0.5, or Ruta recruited).**
Danny's kid escalates from taxing Ruta to a loud incident ON your street —
a smashed stall, a fire barrel, witnesses everywhere: +heat you didn't
earn. Counterplay through built verbs: lean on the kid (nerve check), pay
him off, recruit Ruta and the problem becomes hers to end, or take it to
the Fixer for a price.

**PP4 — Two Lives, One Room (fires: any day-life loyalty ≥ 0.65 AND crew
≥ 1; the guaranteed collision).**
An honored evening (Ada's table, or Noor's) is interrupted by the night
life arriving IN PERSON — a crew member at the door with racket trouble
that cannot wait. Authored: the doorway scene; systemic: who stands there
and what they carry comes from live state. Whatever you choose, both
worlds SAW each other; suspicion and questions follow through the mill.

**PP5 — The Broker's Shop (fires: two arms ≥ 0.5, or player seeks him).**
Halvard opens for business: buy true reads of any arm's state, buy a
meeting with a head, or buy one truce per act. The gossip war gets a
storefront.

**PP6 — The Second Case (fires: Ossei present AND any racket witness
statement taken).**
Ossei connects tonight's street to the warehouse fire — one case now.
Authored: her interview of Lena or Noor (Noor refuses, and TELLS you she
refused — the triangle tightens). Ossei's questions start naming the
rackets, not just the drops.

**PP7 — The Table (fires: any arm reaches stage 4 / attention 0.9).**
The act's summit, routed through Halvard: a sit-down with that arm's head
— Sera's dockside arithmetic, Aldous's courteous ruin, or Danny's
grinning volatility. Offer on the table per doctrine (tribute % / a legal
leash on the fronts / a joint racket you don't control). Accept, defy, or
counter with leverage if you hold the right secrets. The answer sets the
end-state of Act II and the opening pressure of Act III — and, like the
posture, becomes a Fact the street learns.
