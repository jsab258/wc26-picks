B2, THE WINDOWLESS LAUNCH CRASHES ON ITS FIRST LINE. The installer chooses
`pythonw.exe` when one sits beside the interpreter (85 to 96), for the right

here). `print` on a `None` stream returns silently; `.write` and `.flush`

line before `record_refusal` runs, exits 1, and Task Scheduler restarts it

no reader, so its lines go to a file. `launch-supervisor.py` opens

announced), writes its own lines there and to stdout only when stdout is
not `None`, and hands the same handle to the child as stdout and stderr.
`supervise.py` then has a real stream and needs no change to its printing;
its window text becomes the file a reader who is not at the keyboard can
open. Selftest, accepting case first: `main()` with `sys.stdout` set to
`None` on the accepting path spawns exactly once and the log carries the
line; on the refusing path the refusal is recorded and nothing raises; the
spawn kwargs carry the log handle as stdout. Section 9.
