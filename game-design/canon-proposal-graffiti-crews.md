# Canon proposal: graffiti crew tag names

> **STATUS — SPEC**, proposed 2026-09-02, pending Jafar's approval before
> copying into canon.md. Unblocks `G7_graffiti_tags`
> (`production/queue/025-step-4a-seven-images.md`), which needs three to
> five short tag names before the deterministic generator can run.

The em-dash above is required by `tools/docs-check.py`'s STATUS regex; the
rest of this file has none.

## The names

1. **TANNER**, Ironside. The tannery hands' kids took their fathers' trade
   name as their own and spray it on the loading-bay shutters those fathers
   clock into.
2. **SNIDE**, Copper Row. A stallholder's lad who flogs counterfeit gear
   tags himself in the trade's own word for fake, so the market reads it
   as his signature.
3. **GULL**, Gullwing. Kids of the fading resort, named for the birds that
   now outnumber the visitors on the boarded front.
4. **QUAY FIRM**, the Hook. Dockers' sons named for their fathers' street,
   chalked near the ferry queue where they wait for the away coach.
5. **PARADE RATS**, the Parade. Kids too skint for the clubs on the strip,
   wearing the doormen's own name for them as a badge.

## What was deliberately avoided, and why

American graffiti vocabulary: no "crew" as a New York wall uses it, no
"bombing", "piece" or "king". That register is the specific trap the tone
rule names, and this is not New York with rain added.

An invented football club. `QUAY FIRM` borrows the period word for a loose
local following without minting a club canon does not own; that stays a
job for whoever writes the football brand.

Loaded strike-era language, considered for Ironside and dropped: a crew
name should read as a place, not a slogan borrowed from a real dispute.

Anything traceable to a real crew, place or trademark, and any phrasing
that reads as internet-era or as a costume rather than a voice. Each name
is a plain English word a teenager would already use, not one built to
sound like graffiti.

## Note for the director

Nothing else in canon.md changes. The football club, the local paper, the
pirate radio station and the regional TV channel remain unminted, as
canon.md already records.
